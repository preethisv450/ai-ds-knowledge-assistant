# AI & DS Knowledge Assistant — User Guide

## What it does

Answers questions about your course from your own documents: the syllabus for
each subject and the academic regulations. Every answer names the document and
page it came from, so you can check it yourself.

## Getting around

Five buttons along the bottom of the screen, always visible:

**Home** — the starting page, with a card for each feature and an About and help
panel at the foot.

**Module Assistant** — the main study page. Pick your year, semester, subject and
module on the left. Everything else in the app follows that choice.

- *Summary* — writes a structured summary of the module. Tick "Rewrite it" to
  regenerate instead of reusing the saved one. Download it as a text file.
- *Q&A* — ask anything about the module you picked; answers show their sources.
- *Questions* — practice questions, short or long answer, with model answers
  hidden until you click to reveal them.
- *Quiz* — multiple choice. Submit to see your score, the right answers and why.

**Regulation Chatbot** — two modes. *Syllabus* answers from the module selected in
Module Assistant. *Regulation* answers from the rules and policy documents, and
needs no module selected. Clear chat empties the conversation.

**Knowledge Network** — shows how concepts build on each other. Choose a subject
and a module. Arrows point from what to learn first to what it leads to, so read
it top to bottom. Colours are modules. Use + and − to zoom, Fit to see
everything, and drag a box to move it. Hover a concept to see what it needs
first. The panel on the right ranks concepts by how many links they have, and
lists the bridge concepts that the most other topics depend on.

**Timetable** — your weekly class schedule.

## Tips

- Pick your module first. Syllabus answers and all three module tabs depend on it.
- Always open Sources before trusting an answer in an exam context.
- If the app says the service is unavailable, the daily quota is used up. Wait
  and try again.
- Summaries and quizzes are saved per module, so reopening one is instant. Tick
  the rewrite box when you want a fresh one.

## A caution

Answers are generated by an AI model from your course documents. It can be wrong,
and it can miss things. Treat it as a study aid that points you at the right page,
not as the final word. Check the sources.
