# Student feedback — D17

Ask students who have actually used the app for a week, not people seeing it
for the first time. Six to ten responses is enough to see patterns. Put the
questions below in a Google Form, keep it under three minutes.

## The form

**About you**
1. Which year and semester are you in?
2. Which subjects did you use it for?
3. Roughly how many times have you opened it? (once, 2-5, 6-10, more)

**Summaries**
4. Did the summaries cover what you needed to revise? (1-5)
5. Did you find anything in a summary that was wrong or not in your syllabus?
   (free text)
6. Would you revise from a summary instead of your own notes? (yes / no / depends)

**Questions and quizzes**
7. Were the practice questions at the right level? (too easy / about right / too hard)
8. Did any quiz answer look wrong to you? (free text)
9. Did the explanations help you understand why? (1-5)

**Chatbot**
10. Did it answer what you actually asked? (1-5)
11. Did you check the sources it showed? (always / sometimes / never)
12. What did you ask that it could not answer? (free text)

**Knowledge Network**
13. Did the concept map change the order you studied in? (yes / no / did not use it)
14. Was it clear what the arrows meant? (1-5)

**Overall**
15. What is the one thing you would change? (free text)
16. Would you use it next semester? (1-5)
